# About Nudgra

For implementation details, see [Technical Architecture](./docs/technical-architecture.md).
For visual direction and UI rules, see [Design](./DESIGN.md).

## What Nudgra Is

Nudgra is an Instagram automation product built for creators, entrepreneurs, and developers who want the core value of tools like ManyChat without paying an ongoing SaaS subscription for each account they manage.

The idea is simple: the user deploys Nudgra in their own environment, connects their Instagram professional account through Meta, and controls their own automation flows.

## What It Does

Nudgra is meant to automate the most repetitive Instagram messaging work:

- reply automatically to DMs based on keywords
- react to story-related entry points and continue the conversation in DMs
- send short follow-up sequences after someone shows interest
- tag contacts based on intent and interaction
- keep basic visibility over contacts, conversations, and automation activity

## Why It Exists

The problem Nudgra is solving is not that Instagram automation does not exist. It is that the existing tools become expensive fast, especially for people running multiple accounts or trying to keep their operating costs low.

Nudgra is meant to give that operator:

- lower long-term cost
- more control over data and setup
- more flexibility than a closed hosted automation platform

## Who It Is For

Nudgra is for:

- creators selling offers, products, or links through Instagram
- small businesses and operators managing one or more Instagram accounts
- developers who prefer owning the stack instead of renting it

## Product Goal

The goal of Nudgra is to become a practical, low-cost, operator-owned Instagram automation tool that handles the highest-value messaging workflows without unnecessary complexity.

The first goal is not full ManyChat parity.

The first goal is to make one Instagram account usable with:

- keyword-based DM automation
- story-triggered conversation entry
- simple delayed follow-ups
- contact tagging
- a lightweight dashboard for visibility and control

## MVP Goal

A successful MVP should let one operator:

1. connect their own Instagram professional account
2. create a simple automation rule
3. receive inbound events from Meta
4. send automated replies safely inside Meta policy limits
5. review contacts, messages, and failures from the app

## Long-Term Direction

Once the core workflow is stable, Nudgra can expand into:

- multi-account support
- richer automation flows
- comment-to-DM workflows
- better analytics
- public open-source release

## Summary

Nudgra is about ownership, simplicity, and cost control.

It is not trying to be a bloated marketing suite.

It is trying to be a focused Instagram automation product that gives users the essential automation power they need while keeping the stack understandable and under their control.
